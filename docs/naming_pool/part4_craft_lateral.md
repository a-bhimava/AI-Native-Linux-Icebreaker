# Part 4 — Craft, Place & Lateral Metaphor

> Naming pool for the dual-brain architecture.
> Order is always **QB — PB**: the one who *watches/reads/guides* first; the one who *does the work* second.
> Core split: **the one who sees cannot act; the one who acts cannot see.**
