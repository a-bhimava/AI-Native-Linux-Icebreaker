# Naming the Two Brains — The Curated 100

*Second pass. Distilled from a ~1,000-pair research pool (see `dual_brain_naming_1000_pool.md`).*
*Prepared June 17, 2026.*

---

## The brief, restated

Two models, one asymmetry. The **Quarantined Brain (QB)** *perceives* — it faces the user, documents, and the web, but holds zero execution power. The **Privileged Brain (PB)** *acts* — it is blind to the outside world but holds root. The whole security model in one line:

> **The one who sees cannot act; the one who acts cannot see.**

Every pair below is ordered **QB — PB** (the perceiver first, the executor second). The strongest names make a QB that *sounds* lighter / higher / more vocal and a PB that *sounds* heavier / grounded / percussive.

This list was curated for three things at once: **interesting** (no flat "Sensor/Actuator" obviousness), **namable** (a developer can say it, type it, and live with it), and **clean** (low collision risk — though see §3, this is not legal clearance). ⭐ = my strongest picks. ⚠ = verify before adopting.

## 1. The shortlist — 12 I'd actually ship

| Pair (QB — PB) | Family | Why it's strong |
|---|---|---|
| **Mimir — Magni** ⭐ | Norse myth | The severed wise head that *counsels* but cannot act, paired with raw might. Alliterative, evocative, near-collision-free. The flagship. |
| **Sia — Heka** ⭐ | Egyptian | Sia is the literal personification of *perception/insight*; Heka is the personified *executive power that makes magic work*. Two real Egyptian abstractions that ARE the architecture. Short, clean, beautiful. |
| **Thoth — Ptah** ⭐ | Egyptian | The scribe-god of all knowledge vs. the craftsman-god who builds the world. Instantly readable as wisdom→making. |
| **Orunmila — Ogun** ⭐ | Yoruba | The Ifá diviner who *reads destiny* vs. the orisha of iron who *clears the path by force*. Distinctive and dignified. |
| **Drashtri — Kartri** ⭐ | Sanskrit | Literally "the Seer" and "the Doer" (draṣṭṛ / kartṛ). The pure philosophical dyad, in a language that makes it sound designed. |
| **Verifier — Prover** ⭐ | Cryptography | The verifier only *checks*; the prover *constructs* and never validates itself. Native security-protocol vocabulary — perfect for this product. |
| **Handler — Asset** ⭐ | Espionage | The handler *perceives and tasks*; the asset *acts in the field, blind to the whole*. Maps the trust boundary exactly, in two everyday words. |
| **Afferent — Efferent** ⭐ | Neuroanatomy | Nerves carrying signal *inward* vs. *outward to muscle*. Technically perfect; an elegant deep cut. |
| **Dial — Escapement** ⭐ | Horology | The dial *shows* but moves nothing; the escapement *releases the train* it never displays. Quietly gorgeous. |
| **Casement — Crucible** ⭐ | Architecture | A window that opens to *look* vs. the blind *melt*. Great mouth-feel, vivid contrast. |
| **Väinämöinen — Ilmarinen** ⭐ | Kalevala | The all-knowing singer-seer vs. the eternal smith who forged the Sampo. Epic; a little long but unforgettable. |
| **Videns — Faber** ⭐ | Latin | "The seeing one" / "the craftsman." Reads like a real codename pair, self-documenting to anyone with a little Latin. |

**If I had to pick one:** **Mimir / Magni** for story, **Sia / Heka** for elegance, **Verifier / Prover** for a security audience that will *get it* immediately.

## 3. Collision notes (preliminary screen — not legal clearance)

Most picks here are deliberately deep cuts to dodge the obvious traps from the first pass (Oracle, Vault, Cortex, Sentinel, Janus, Heimdall, etc., all avoided). Residual cautions worth a real USPTO/GitHub/package check before you commit:

- **Lookout** — a mobile-security company; fine as a role-word, risky as the product brand. ⚠
- **Sarutahiko / Inari, Itzamna / Kukulkan, Qianliyan / Erlang** — mythologically clean, but "Erlang" collides hard with the programming language; prefer the others if that matters. ⚠
- **Aurora** — widely used (AWS Aurora, others); the *pair* "Aurora — Anvil" is distinctive but check the standalone word. ⚠
- **Kalman, Governor, Observer, Operator, Handler, Asset, Verifier, Prover** — common nouns, not brands, but exactly because they're common they're weak trademarks; great as internal role-names, harder to own as a public product mark.
- **Talos** (appeared in the pool) was deliberately *excluded* here — it collides with Cisco Talos. **Argus** likewise (Argus network monitor) was demoted.

Mythological deep cuts (Mimir/Magni, Sia/Heka, Thoth/Ptah, Orunmila/Ogun, Drashtri/Kartri, Väinämöinen/Ilmarinen) carry the lowest tech-namespace risk and are the safest to build a brand around.

---

## 4. Recommendation

Lead candidate: **Mimir — Magni** (story + clean) or **Sia — Heka** (elegance + clean). For a name your security-minded users will instantly decode, **Verifier — Prover** or **Handler — Asset**. Keep **Q / P** as the internal shorthand regardless — it maps straight to the Quarantined/Privileged vocabulary already in the code.

Whatever you choose, run the formal trademark + registry check in §3 on the final pair before it ships.
