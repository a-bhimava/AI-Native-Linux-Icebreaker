# Part 1 — Myth, Religion & Esoterica

Name pairs for the dual-brain architecture. Always **QB — PB**: the perceiver/seer/messenger first (sees but cannot act), the executor/maker/guardian second (acts but cannot see).

> The poetic core: *The one who sees cannot act; the one who acts cannot see.*
