# Part 3 — Language, Etymology & Coined Words

Name pairs for the dual-brain architecture. Always ordered **QB — PB**: the **Quarantined Brain** (perceiver — aware but powerless, faces the untrusted outside, zero execution) first; the **Privileged Brain** (maker — blind but holds root, does all real action) second.

Core conceit: *the one who sees cannot act; the one who acts cannot see.*

Root/language abbreviations: **L** Latin, **Gk** Ancient Greek, **OE** Old English, **ON** Old Norse, **Skt** Sanskrit, **PIE** Proto-Indo-European, **†** coined/portmanteau, **⚠** check for trademark collision.

## Notes on Use

- Items marked **†** are coined; verify they read as intended and aren't accidentally rude in other languages before shipping.
- Items marked **⚠** brush against existing tech products, mythic brands, or common terms (e.g. *Logos*, *Argus*, *Kernel*, *Saga*) — run a trademark and namespace check before adopting.
- The architecture's asymmetry is best served when the QB name *sounds lighter, higher, more vocal/luminous* and the PB name *sounds heavier, grounded, percussive/ferrous*. Pairs in §9, §11, and §18 lean hardest into that phonaesthetic contrast.
